# Northstar Foods - Line PKG-07 Safety Procedures

**Document ID:** SAFE-PKG07-001  
**Revision:** Demo 1.0  
**Applies to:** Northstar Foods packaging line PKG-07  
**Status:** Fictional demonstration document - not approved for real-world operations

## 1. Purpose and authority

This compact procedure defines the demo plant's minimum rules for safe operation, inspection, jam clearing, cleaning, and maintenance of packaging line PKG-07. It is designed for an AI prototype and does not replace an employer's site-specific energy-control program, training, equipment manuals, hazard assessment, or applicable law.

Only trained **authorized maintenance employees** may apply lockout/tagout (LOTO) and perform work that exposes them to hazardous energy. **Affected employees**, including operators and floor supervisors, may stop the line, report a condition, and follow normal operating controls, but they may not perform maintenance behind guards unless separately authorized.

## 2. Equipment and hazards

| Equipment ID | Equipment | Principal hazards |
|---|---|---|
| CV-07 | Infeed conveyor | Nip points, entanglement, unexpected movement, stored belt tension |
| FL-07 | Auger filler | Rotating auger, pneumatic movement, product dust |
| SL-07 | Heat sealer | Hot surfaces, pinch points, electrical energy, pneumatic pressure |
| LB-07 | Labeler/coder | Rollers, pinch points, hot print head |
| CK-07 | Checkweigher/reject station | Moving belt, pneumatic reject arm |

Energy sources on PKG-07 may include 480 V line power, 120 V control power, compressed air, gravity, hot surfaces, rotating components, and stored mechanical tension. Pressing an emergency stop or using a software stop does **not** isolate these energy sources.

## 3. Stop-work and emergency response

Any employee shall stop the line when there is an immediate risk of injury, smoke or burning odor, exposed moving parts, a missing or defeated guard, uncontrolled product leakage, sparking, or an unexpected restart.

1. Press the nearest emergency stop if immediate motion must stop.
2. Warn nearby personnel and notify the floor supervisor.
3. Do not reach through a guard, step over a conveyor, or attempt a restart.
4. For fire, injury, electrical arcing, or uncontrolled release, follow the site's emergency response plan and contact emergency services as applicable.
5. Preserve the scene unless doing so creates additional risk.

An emergency stop is a protective action, not an energy-isolation procedure. Maintenance or entry into a danger zone still requires the equipment-specific LOTO procedure.

## 4. Machine guarding and normal operation

- Operate PKG-07 only when fixed guards, access doors, and interlocks are installed and functional.
- Never bypass, tape, defeat, or hold an interlock closed.
- Do not place hands, tools, clothing, or cleaning materials inside a guarded area while equipment is energized.
- Operators may make adjustments only from designated external controls and only when the applicable operating procedure explicitly permits them.
- A visual inspection from outside intact guarding is permitted during normal operation. Opening a guard or entering a danger zone changes the activity to servicing and requires energy control.
- If a guard or interlock is damaged, stop the affected equipment, label it **OUT OF SERVICE**, and notify Maintenance.

## 5. Lockout/tagout decision rule

Apply the equipment-specific LOTO procedure before servicing or maintenance when unexpected energization, startup, or release of stored energy could cause injury. Examples include:

- removing or bypassing a guard;
- reaching into a conveyor, filler, sealer, labeler, or reject station;
- clearing a jam inside a danger zone;
- adjusting belt tracking, tension, pulleys, chains, sensors, or internal components;
- cleaning near exposed moving parts;
- inspecting an overheating motor at close range or removing its fan cover;
- repairing pneumatic, electrical, or thermal systems.

If there is uncertainty about whether LOTO applies, stop and escalate to the authorized maintenance lead. Production pressure is not a reason to omit energy control.

## 6. Standard LOTO sequence for PKG-07

Only an authorized employee may perform this sequence using the current equipment-specific energy-isolation sheet.

1. **Prepare:** Identify the equipment, all energy sources, stored energy, isolation points, and affected personnel.
2. **Notify:** Tell affected employees which equipment will be shut down and why.
3. **Orderly shutdown:** Use the normal stop control to prevent additional hazards or product release.
4. **Isolate:** Open the identified electrical disconnects; close and secure pneumatic supplies; block or restrain gravity and stored mechanical energy; allow hot surfaces to cool.
5. **Lock and tag:** Each authorized employee applies a personal lock and identification tag to each required isolation device or group lockbox.
6. **Dissipate stored energy:** Bleed pressure, discharge or restrain stored energy, and verify that it cannot reaccumulate to a hazardous level.
7. **Verify zero-energy state:** Attempt a normal start using safe positioning, check appropriate indicators or test instruments, and return controls to OFF. Verification must occur before work begins.
8. **Perform work:** Maintain control of keys and keep guards/barriers appropriate to the task.
9. **Inspect before release:** Confirm tools and loose parts are removed, components and guards are operationally intact, and employees are clear.
10. **Remove locks and notify:** Each employee normally removes their own lock. Restore energy in a controlled manner and notify affected employees before restart.

Temporary energization for testing may occur only under the authorized test-positioning procedure: clear tools and people, remove locks under the approved process, energize only for the test, de-energize, and reapply full energy control before continuing work.

## 7. Jam clearing

### 7.1 External, no-entry correction

An operator may use a manufacturer-provided external control or fixed clearing device only when all of the following are true:

- no guard is opened or bypassed;
- no body part or handheld tool enters a danger zone;
- the task is documented as a normal operating action;
- effective guarding remains in place; and
- the condition can be corrected without exposure to unexpected movement.

If any condition is not met, stop and use LOTO.

### 7.2 Guarded-area jam

For a pouch, label, or product jam inside guarded equipment:

1. Stop the affected machine and upstream feed.
2. Notify the supervisor and Maintenance.
3. Apply the equipment-specific LOTO procedure.
4. Release stored pneumatic, thermal, gravity, and mechanical energy.
5. Use the designated tool after zero-energy verification; never use hands where hidden tension may release.
6. Inspect guards and sensors before restoring energy.
7. Quality must identify and hold potentially affected product under QC-PKG07-001 Section 8.

## 8. Overheating, unusual noise, vibration, or belt drift

If CV-07 or its motor shows overheating, smoke, burning odor, repeated overload trips, unusual noise, excessive vibration, belt-edge rubbing, or visible belt drift:

- Stop feeding product and perform an orderly line stop.
- Do not open the guard, touch the motor, or run the conveyor solely to "see if it clears."
- If there is smoke, arcing, or immediate danger, use the emergency stop and emergency response procedure.
- Establish an exclusion area around hot or moving equipment.
- Notify authorized Maintenance and provide the alarm, time, load condition, and observations.
- Maintenance shall follow MAINT-PKG07-001. Guard removal, close inspection, belt adjustment, and internal cleaning require LOTO.
- Product exposed after the last acceptable quality check shall be handled under QC-PKG07-001.

## 9. Cleaning and spills

- Stop the line before routine external cleaning. Use only approved cleaning agents and required PPE from the chemical label and site hazard assessment.
- Never direct water at electrical cabinets, motors not approved for washdown, controls, or energized connections.
- Cleaning beneath or behind guards, removing conveyor parts, or placing hands near hazardous motion requires LOTO.
- Mark and contain floor spills promptly. Keep aisles and access to emergency stops and disconnects clear.
- After cleaning, inspect for tools, loose parts, pooled liquid, displaced guards, and chemical residue before release.
- Quality or Sanitation must document the pre-operational inspection before food-contact operation resumes.

## 10. Safe restart after maintenance

The authorized maintenance employee controls energy restoration. Before restart:

- the work area is inspected and all employees are safely positioned;
- tools and nonessential items are removed;
- guards, interlocks, fasteners, sensors, and covers are restored;
- each personal lock is removed by the person who applied it, except under the site's documented exceptional-removal procedure;
- affected employees are notified;
- the machine is started in controlled/jog mode where the approved procedure permits;
- Maintenance verifies basic function under MAINT-PKG07-001 Section 9; and
- Quality completes the restart and product-release checks in QC-PKG07-001 Sections 5 and 7.

Do not release product merely because the machine runs. Safety release, equipment-function verification, and quality release are distinct decisions.

## 11. Escalation triggers

Stop and escalate when:

- the equipment ID or energy-isolation point is uncertain;
- a required lockable isolation device is unavailable or defective;
- an interlock or guard does not function;
- stored energy cannot be verified as dissipated;
- overheating, tripping, vibration, or drift recurs after one authorized corrective action;
- product may be contaminated or incorrectly labeled; or
- instructions conflict across documents.

Safety requirements take precedence over production and troubleshooting convenience. The supervisor may keep equipment out of service but may not authorize bypassing a safety control.
